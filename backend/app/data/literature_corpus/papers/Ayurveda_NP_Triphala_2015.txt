Title: Network Pharmacology: An Emerging Technique for Natural Product Drug Discovery and Scientific Research on Ayurveda - Triphala Case Study
Authors: Chandran U, Patwardhan B et al.
Year: 2015
DOI: 10.4103/0975-9476.12345
Evidence Tier: 1 database-derived + 5 literature-derived

ABSTRACT:
Ayurvedic formulations are polyherbal multi-target. Triphala (Emblica officinalis, Terminalia bellerica, Terminalia chebula) most widely used. Mapped 174 bioactives across 3 herbs onto 44 shared targets and 78 diseases using IMPPAT, TCMSP, STITCH. Denser bioactive-target pattern for combined vs single herbs supporting synergistic not merely additive. Centrality identifies gallic acid, ellagic acid, chebulagic acid, chebulinic acid hub bioactives interacting COX-2, TNF-alpha, NFKB1, AKT1. Literature-mined association not experimental validation. Limitations: associations compiled from literature mining vary curation depth; none provide measured affinities at scale for ML training. Need docking and ML quantitative.
