Title: Insights about multi-targeting and synergistic neuromodulators in Ayurvedic herbs against epilepsy
Authors: Sharma H, Rao VS, Jain A 2019 Frontiers PMC6646331
Evidence Tier: 1+2

ABSTRACT:
Epilepsy 50M globally 30% resistant. Ayurveda 63 anti-epileptic herbs centuries empirical use but molecular mechanisms uncharacterised. Integrated NP with molecular docking screened 349 drug-like phytochemicals filtered from 63 herbs using IMPPAT ADMET + Lipinski against mGluR2 PDB 7E9G/5CNI and mGluR3 5CNK. Similarity to DrugBank anti-epileptics levetiracetam lamotrigine validation heuristic. Vina docking with PLIP identified 11 novel neuromodulator candidates and 74 phytochemicals with polypharmacological similarity to approved compounds. Top: bacoside A Bacopa monnieri -8.2 kcal/mol vs mGluR2 withanolide A Withania somnifera -7.9 H-bonds ARG-107 SER-278. Closest precedent to proposed pipeline but confined single target class and lacks downstream ML XAI literature validation.
